Drop the following Recoleta font files in this directory:

  Recoleta-Regular.woff2
  Recoleta-Bold.woff2

Recoleta is a commercial typeface by Latinotype (latinotype.com).
Purchase and download from: https://www.latinotype.com/fonts/recoleta

After downloading, convert .otf/.ttf to .woff2:
  npx ttf2woff2 Recoleta-Regular.otf Recoleta-Regular.woff2
  npx ttf2woff2 Recoleta-Bold.otf Recoleta-Bold.woff2

(Old GT Super files can be deleted from this directory.)
